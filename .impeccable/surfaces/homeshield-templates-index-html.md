## Surface

**Target:** homeshield/templates/index.html
**Mode:** Operate
**Audience:** Non-technical homeowner or parent checking safety from a phone; mixed indoor/outdoor light.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A departure-board operating interface where every new event cascades into its column one character at a time — TIME, LOCATION, EVENT, CONFIDENCE resolving independently, like a live Solari board. Refuses the ops-center glass-and-glow aesthetic: no blur, no gradient borders, no monospace as costume. The board earns authority through typographic discipline and materiality, not heroics.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ground `#16213e` (warm matte navy). Display: Chakra Petch condensed (a face built for electronic readout boards). Body: Rubik. Hairline column rules (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.10)`) as structural skeleton. Three signal states only — amber `#f5a200` (attention/active), signal red `#e8002a` (critical/alert), spring green `#00d68f` (clear/online). No glass, no gradients, no glow, no colored drop-shadows. Camera cards: uniform fixed-width tiles in a horizontal strip, same scale across all.

**STORY:** A parent opens the dashboard. The board is already live — column headers resolved, rows filled with recent events. They scan for red. No red: household safe. Red row: act on it. Each row tells them when, where, and what — in that order. The confidence label tells them whether to believe it. Trust is earned by the board never lying to them.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-height Concourse board fills the viewport. Slim nav strip at top: HomeShield wordmark left (Chakra Petch), status pill right. Below: the board — hairline-ruled columns TIME / LOCATION / EVENT / CONFIDENCE stretching the full width. Column headers in small tracked uppercase Chakra Petch. Rows in Rubik at comfortable body size. Active camera strip below the board in a compact scrollable row of uniform tiles. On mobile: single column stacked layout, board takes full width, camera tiles scroll horizontally below.

**FORM:** Split-flap concourse board (challenger 6, seed `16a27450`). Won both axes (audience identification and product clarity) against the assigned direction (illustrated home atlas, candidate 7). Raises from declined challengers: monochromatic total commitment (from Factory Records direction), consistent camera scale across tiles (from Botanical folio), timeline spine / column grid (from Labanotation notation). Honest risk: dark ground — the entire world distinction lives in typographic discipline and motion execution, not materials.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
